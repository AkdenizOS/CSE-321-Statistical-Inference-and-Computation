# Topic 4 — Discrete Random Variables

**Previous:** [Topic 3](03-probability.md) · **Next:** [Topic 5](05-continuous-random-variables.md)

## Goals
- Work with a discrete probability distribution and its mean and variance.
- Recognize and apply the binomial and Poisson models.

## Key concepts
- Discrete random variable; probability distribution `p(x)` with `Σp(x)=1`.
- `μ = E(X) = Σ x·p(x)`; `σ² = E[(X−μ)²] = Σ(x−μ)²p(x)`.
- **Binomial**: `n` identical independent trials, two outcomes, constant `p`. `p(x) = C(n,x)pˣq^(n−x)`, `μ=np`, `σ²=npq`.
- **Poisson**: rare events over an interval. `p(x)=λˣe^(−λ)/x!`, `μ=σ²=λ`.
- **Hypergeometric**: sampling *without* replacement — this is what makes it not binomial.
- Check the four binomial conditions before using it; that check is usually worth marks.
