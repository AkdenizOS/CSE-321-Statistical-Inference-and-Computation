# Topic 6 — Sampling Distributions

**Previous:** [Topic 5](05-continuous-random-variables.md) · **Next:** [Topic 7](07-confidence-intervals.md)

## Goals
- State and apply the Central Limit Theorem.
- Describe the sampling distribution of the sample mean.

## Key concepts
- A **statistic is a random variable** — its distribution is the sampling distribution. This is the conceptual hinge of the course.
- Unbiased estimator: `E(x̄) = μ`. The sample variance divides by `n−1` precisely to be unbiased.
- Sampling distribution of `x̄`: mean `μ`, standard error `σ/√n`.
- **Central Limit Theorem**: for large `n` (rule of thumb `n ≥ 30`), `x̄` is approximately normal *regardless of the population's shape*.
- If the population is already normal, `x̄` is exactly normal for any `n`.
