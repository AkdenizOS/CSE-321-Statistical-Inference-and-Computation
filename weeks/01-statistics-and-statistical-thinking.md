# Topic 1 — Statistics, Data, and Statistical Thinking

**Next:** [Topic 2](02-describing-sets-of-data.md)

## Goals
- Say what statistics is for and where the numbers come from.
- Tell a population from a sample, and a parameter from a statistic.

## Key concepts
- **Population** vs. **sample**; **parameter** (population) vs. **statistic** (sample).
- Descriptive vs. inferential statistics — describing what you have vs. concluding about what you do not.
- Variable types: qualitative (categorical) vs. quantitative (numeric).
- Sampling: representative samples, random sampling, selection bias, nonresponse bias.
- A statistical inference is incomplete without a **measure of reliability**.
