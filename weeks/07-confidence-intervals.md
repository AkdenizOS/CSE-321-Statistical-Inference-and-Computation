# Topic 7 — Estimation with Confidence Intervals

**Previous:** [Topic 6](06-sampling-distributions.md) · **Next:** [Topic 8](08-hypothesis-testing.md)

## Goals
- Build a confidence interval for a mean or a proportion.
- Determine the sample size needed for a target margin of error.

## Key concepts
- Point estimate vs. **interval estimate**; confidence coefficient.
- Large sample CI for `μ`: `x̄ ± z_(α/2)·σ/√n` (use `s` when `σ` is unknown and `n` is large).
- Small sample CI for `μ`: `x̄ ± t_(α/2)·s/√n` with `n−1` degrees of freedom; requires an approximately normal population.
- **When to use z vs. t** — the most common exam mistake.
- CI for a proportion `p`: `p̂ ± z_(α/2)√(p̂q̂/n)`.
- Sample size: `n = (z_(α/2)σ/E)²` for a margin of error `E`.
- '95% confident' describes the procedure, not the particular interval.
