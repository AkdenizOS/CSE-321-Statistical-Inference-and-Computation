# Topic 8 — Tests of Hypothesis

**Previous:** [Topic 7](07-confidence-intervals.md)

## Goals
- Set up and carry out a hypothesis test, and state the conclusion correctly.
- Interpret a p-value and distinguish Type I from Type II error.

## Key concepts
- `H₀` and `Hₐ`; the burden of proof is on `Hₐ`.
- **Type I error** (reject a true `H₀`, probability `α`) vs. **Type II** (fail to reject a false `H₀`, probability `β`). Power `= 1 − β`.
- Test statistic, rejection region, one-tailed vs. two-tailed.
- **p-value** = probability of a result at least as extreme as the observed one, *given `H₀` is true*. Reject when `p ≤ α`.
- We **never accept `H₀`** — only fail to reject it. Exams check this wording.
- Tests for `μ` (large sample z, small sample t) and for `p`.
- The duality: a two-tailed test at `α` rejects exactly when the `(1−α)` CI excludes the hypothesized value.
