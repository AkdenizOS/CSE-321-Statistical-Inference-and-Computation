# Topic 2 — Methods for Describing Sets of Data

**Previous:** [Topic 1](01-statistics-and-statistical-thinking.md) · **Next:** [Topic 3](03-probability.md)

## Goals
- Build and read the standard graphical summaries.
- Compute and interpret measures of centre, spread and relative standing.

## Key concepts
- Graphs: bar chart and pie chart (qualitative); dot plot, stem-and-leaf, histogram, box plot (quantitative).
- Centre: **mean**, **median**, **mode**. The median resists outliers, the mean does not.
- Spread: range, **variance** `s²`, **standard deviation** `s`. Divide by `n−1` for the sample variance.
- **Empirical rule**: for a mound-shaped distribution, ≈68% / 95% / 99.7% within 1 / 2 / 3 SD.
- **Chebyshev's rule**: at least `1 − 1/k²` within `k` SD, for *any* distribution.
- Relative standing: percentiles, **z-score**. Outlier detection with the 1.5·IQR rule.
- Skewness: where the mean sits relative to the median.
